## Choose the challenge, not just the activity

A corporate team may need more sustainable work rhythms. A school may need educators who feel equipped to support students. A resort may need a commercially sound wellness identity. An NGO may need an accessible community programme that continues after external facilitation ends.

These are different problems with different buyers, risks and measures of progress. That is why Dīpātma maintains six specialised solution portfolios instead of offering the same workshop everywhere.

Impact Advisory is for organisations that need to understand the likely effects of a decision or design for a specific community outcome. It is available on its own or alongside a sector engagement. Start with a Community Impact Strategy, Decision Impact Review or readiness scan.

## Three engagement formats. One accountable process.

**Consulting**: Assess priorities, create strategies, design service systems and develop implementation plans.

**Training**: Build relevant capability through facilitated programmes, practical tools and follow-up resources.

**Strategic partnerships**: Co-create, activate and refine programmes across an agreed engagement period.

A project may involve one format or progress through all three. Every proposal defines the work involved, the deliverables, responsibilities and the review process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Dīpātma offer pre-designed programmes?

We maintain signature service frameworks, but scope and delivery are adapted to the audience, location, operating realities and agreed objectives.

### Can a project involve more than one sector?

Yes. For example, a hospitality property may need both guest experience design and staff training, or a foundation may operate an educational programme. We can shape one coordinated scope across relevant portfolios.

### Do you offer implementation support after designing a programme?

Yes, subject to the agreed engagement. Support may include pilots, team training, operational documentation, periodic review and an ongoing advisory arrangement.
