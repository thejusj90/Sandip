## What happens after you enquire?

**01 — Review:** We examine the organisation, intended audience, broad needs and requested support.

**02 — Fit conversation:** If appropriate, we discuss scope, decision-makers, location, timeline and any information needed for a proposal.

**03 — Defined next step:** Depending on the requirement, we may propose a diagnostic, scoped training, programme-design assignment or partnership discussion.

An introductory conversation does not include free proprietary programme design or a commitment to accept the project.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can we enquire without a confirmed budget?

Yes. It helps to share your intended scope and decision-making stage, even if the budget is still being considered. Detailed design and site assessments are separately scoped where applicable.

### Do you provide presentations for organisational decision-makers?

A suitable capabilities conversation or scoped proposal can be arranged after an initial fit review. Detailed technical or commercial designs are commissioned work.

### Can we partner on a resort or retreat experience?

Yes. We can consider design advisory, named-programme delivery and longer-term co-created experiences, depending on property suitability and commercial alignment.

### Can you undertake projects outside Goa?

Potentially. We review delivery format, scheduling, travel and local specialist requirements before confirming a scope.
