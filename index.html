The FullChoice
Breath Cycle™

A simple, science-backed cycle that connects breath, body and choice—anytime, anywhere. Five navigable resolutions. Real change lived.

Explore the Cycle
↓1. Release

Reduce unnecessary abdominal, shoulder and facial holding.

↑↑2. Expand

Begin a slow diaphragmatic-led inhalation.

↗3. Complete

Continue towards comfortable fullness without force.

≈4. Allow

Let exhalation release naturally and comfortably.

◉5. Choose

Notice the internal state before the next thought, word or action.
