ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Sandip Jadhav

FullChoice Breath is developed through the synthesis of more than a decade of interdisciplinary and cross-cultural wellness practice.

Sandip observed how we breathe may influence more than relaxation, it can create a useful moment of awareness before habitual response. His work brings together practical experience across breath, yoga and holistic wellness with an evolving evidence-informed framework for self-regulation and conscious response.
